## Review rule

`seedsRead` and `seedsWrite` in `ambiguousEvent()` are metadata fields. They do not, by themselves, alter eligibility, outcome weights or effects. A canonical function that says a seed **modifies** a future scene therefore needs explicit gate/modifier/transition/effect evidence; listing the seed in `seedsRead` is not sufficient.

Likewise, a compound canonical condition represented by a single runtime flag remains `needs_upstream_proof` until the writer of that flag is shown to encode the required causal condition.

## Important concrete gap: `CEVT_19_SOCIAL_01`

This is the clearest direct semantic mismatch in the 18–20 exact-ID group.

Canonical condition:
- `FLAG_NIGHT_PHOTO` **or** high exposure + recent conflict.

Runtime condition:
- `NIGHT_PHOTO == true`
- **and** `mediaHeat >= 15`
- no recent-conflict requirement.

The current runtime is therefore both narrower on the photo route and missing the alternative exposure/conflict route. This should be repaired before full certification.
