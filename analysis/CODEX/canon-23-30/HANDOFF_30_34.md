# Handoff to 30–34

The 30–34 owner must receive authoritative state, not narrative guesses.

Required handoff fields/concepts:

- club, owner club, registration club and route;
- contract months, salary, release clause and pending CareerOffer;
- role, minutes/form and recent authoritative sport facts;
- captain slot separately from generic leadership/locker influence;
- certified active agent, when available;
- national-team status/history only when authoritative;
- injury history and recovery state;
- family/home/wealth state;
- public/media reputation and polarization;
- persistent NPC relationships and knowledge;
- live plus terminal/historical seeds with original `originEvent` preserved;
- age-30 priority and positional-reinvention memory.

## Age-30 priority contract

`EVT_29_FIN_001` is staged as the canonical five-choice Pasada-5 close. Its persisted `world.age30Priority` tokens are:

- `maximum` — keep competing at the highest level even with fewer minutes;
- `minutes` — remain important every week;
- `body` — protect body, family and stability;
- `freedom` — preserve freedom to move/reinvent;
- `legacy` — prioritize coherence of legacy over maximizing another variable.

The corresponding `SEED_AGE30_PRIORITY` payload stores the same token with `advisory=true`. The priority is guidance, not a route lock. The 30–34 layer may use it for wording, recommendations or bounded weights, but must not remove otherwise valid choices solely because the player later contradicts it.

Compatibility: historical saves may already contain `legacy`, `minutes`, `body` or `freedom` from the old four-choice placeholder. Those values remain valid and must not be rewritten. `maximum` is additive for new canonical resolutions.

Forbidden handoff shortcuts: `age >= 30` does not mean veteran leader; reputation does not mean captain; national standing does not mean called up; market heat does not mean offer; old-club actors do not remain current institutional actors after a move.
